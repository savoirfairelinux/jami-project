Tucnet: offline mesh chat on top of Jami

Four screens in the look of your reference (lavender home, warm full-bleed chat, neon room), redrawn around mesh features. Below them: the architecture, libraries per module, platform limits and a phased roadmap. "Tucnet" is a working name taken from the repository.

Architecture

Five layers, one portable core

Your repository is a Jami fork: a C++ daemon shared by a Kotlin Android client and a Swift iOS client. Jami already has account-free identity and encrypted calls and chats over the internet. The mesh is added beside that path as a new set of bearers, with the protocol logic written once in C++ so both platforms speak the identical wire format.

A relay only ever sees: message ID, a recipient hint that rotates daily, hop limit, expiry and ciphertext. There is no sender field in the clear.

Data flow

A message to someone out of range

  1. Encrypt. Alice's phone encrypts for Carlo with the ratchet key for this one message.
  2. Address. The core adds a random ID, Carlo's hint, 7 hops and a 24 h expiry.
  3. Pick a bearer. Direct BLE, then Wi-Fi Aware, then relay, then LoRa. Carlo is not nearby, so relay.
  4. Flood. Sent to every neighbour over BLE. Each drops duplicates, stores the ciphertext and forwards.
  5. Carry. Ben, a stranger, walks away holding it. He cannot read it.
  6. Detect. Hours later Ben's scanner sees a BLE advert with Carlo's hint and connects.
  7. Deliver. Carlo decrypts and signs a receipt.
  8. Clean up. The receipt floods back; carriers delete their copies and Alice sees Delivered.

Anything over about 64 KB negotiates a Wi-Fi Aware link over BLE first, then streams 256 KB hashed chunks that resume by index.

Limits

Where the hardware and OS push back

Android kills background apps

Doze and vendor battery managers (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Huawei) stop the mesh once the app is swiped away.

Foreground service with a persistent notification, PendingIntent BLE scans, an onboarding step for the battery exemption.

Wi-Fi Aware is not everywhere

Many budget phones lack it, and it often cannot run beside a hotspot or more than one or two data paths.

Check the feature at runtime, fall back to Wi-Fi Direct, one bulk transfer at a time.

iPhones relay poorly in the background

iOS hides the advertised service and slows scans when the app is not open. No fast link to Android before iOS 26.

BLE across platforms, state restoration, and say plainly in the UI that an iPhone relays best while open.

BLE holds few links

A phone manages roughly 4–7 connections, and constant scanning costs battery.

Connect, sync, disconnect. Low-power scanning by default and a visible relay-mode switch.

X3DH assumes a server

One-time prekeys cannot be handed out safely by gossip, so a first message can be replayed.

One-time keys only by QR or direct contact; dedup on message ID; ratchet immediately. Get an outside audit before release.

LoRa is tiny and regulated

About 200 bytes per packet, seconds of airtime, country-specific frequency and duty-cycle rules.

Text only, rate-limited, and the radio's own region setting stays in charge.

Sideloading friction

Play Protect warnings, the unknown-apps switch, and Google's incoming developer verification for sideloaded apps.

Register the signing identity, show the certificate fingerprint on the download page, keep an F-Droid build.

Metadata still leaks

BLE presence is visible to anyone nearby, and a relay next to the sender can guess who is talking.

Rotating hints, no sender in the header, encrypted hops. Describe the remaining exposure to users.

Roadmap

Seven phases, Android first

Durations are rough estimates for two or three developers.

0 · Spike 2 weeks

Initialise the Jami submodules, build the Android client, test which target phones have Wi-Fi Aware and L2CAP, set up the new theme.

two phones exchange raw bytes over BLE and over Aware.

1 · MVP 6–8 weeks

libmesh skeleton, on-device identity, add contact by QR, BLE discovery and link, 1:1 text with X3DH + Double Ratchet, Nearby and Chat screens.

two phones in airplane mode hold an encrypted chat.

2 · Mesh and store-and-forward 6 weeks

Flooding router, dedup, 24 h store, receipts, foreground service, battery tuning.

A reaches C through B, and a message survives a 6-hour gap.

3 · High-speed transfer 6 weeks

Wi-Fi Aware upgrade, chunked files, images, voice notes, Wi-Fi Direct fallback.

a 100 MB file transfers directly and resumes after a break.

4 · Reach 4–6 weeks

Offline APK sharing and the Meshtastic bridge, BLE first then USB.

a fresh phone installs from a peer offline; text crosses 1 km through two radios.

5 · On-device AI 6 weeks

Transcription, translation, local filtering, model packs shared over the mesh.

a voice note is transcribed and translated in airplane mode on a mid-range phone.

6 · Scale ongoing

Encrypted rooms, smarter routing, iOS client, security audit, Jami Swarm sync over the mesh.

audit findings are closed and a 20-phone field test passes.
